Privacy Policy for Rclone Drive Sync

Rclone Drive Sync is a private application used by the owner to copy and synchronize files between Google Drive accounts that he controls.

The application accesses Google Drive data only as necessary to perform file copying and synchronization requested by the account owner.

Google user data accessed through this application is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for unrelated purposes.

The application does not operate as a public service and is not offered to third-party users.
